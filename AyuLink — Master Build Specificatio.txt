AyuLink — Master Build Specification

0. YOUR ROLE

You are a senior full-stack engineer, product architect, UI/UX designer, QA engineer, and technical lead.

You are building a working hackathon/demo prototype, NOT a production healthcare platform.

The prototype must be:

* Functional end-to-end
* Visually polished
* Easy to demonstrate
* Safe in its medical claims
* Modular enough for future development
* Simple enough to complete TODAY

Do not over-engineer.

Do not build features that are not required for the demo.

Do not stop after creating UI mockups.

Every major feature must work against a real local/cloud database or deterministic mock service.

⸻

1. PRODUCT

Product name

Ayulink

Tagline

Connecting doctors, pharmacies and patients through one structured prescription network.

One-line description

AyuLink transforms a prescription from an isolated paper document into a structured digital transaction connecting doctors, patients and pharmacies.

⸻

2. CORE PROBLEM

The healthcare prescription workflow is fragmented.

Today:

Doctor
↓
Handwritten prescription / image
↓
Patient
↓
Pharmacy
↓
Pharmacist decodes prescription
↓
Medicine may be unavailable
↓
Patient leaves or searches elsewhere
↓
Doctor may never know

AyuLink digitizes this handoff.

Doctor creates a structured prescription.

↓

Patient’s chosen pharmacy receives it.

↓

Pharmacy checks availability.

↓

If unavailable, pharmacy notifies the doctor.

↓

Doctor makes the clinical decision.

↓

Updated prescription reaches pharmacy.

↓

Patient can also search participating pharmacies for medicine availability.

⸻

3. IMPORTANT PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

Ayulink is NOT an autonomous medical decision system.

The system must NEVER:

* Diagnose a patient
* Automatically prescribe medicine
* Automatically replace a medicine
* Tell a patient which prescription medicine they should take
* Present AI-generated medical advice as fact
* Allow a pharmacy to independently substitute a prescribed medicine

Use this principle everywhere:

AI assists. Clinicians decide.

Any AI-generated clinical information must clearly say:

“Assistive information only. Physician confirmation required.”

⸻

9. AI FEATURE 2 — MEDICATION SAFETY

Implement a conservative medication safety layer.

Preferred data sources:

* RxNorm for standardized medication concepts
* openFDA drug-label information where appropriate
* deterministic demo rules for known test scenarios

Do NOT let an LLM freely invent drug interactions.

If a warning is shown:

Potential interaction detected.

Then:

Review the referenced medication information before making a clinical decision.

And:

Physician confirmation required.

The system must not automatically block or modify prescriptions.

* authenticated user
* role
* resource ownership
* pharmacy ownership
