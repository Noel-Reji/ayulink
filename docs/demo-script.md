# AyuLink Master Demo Presentation Script

Follow this step-by-step presentation script to demonstrate AyuLink's core capabilities in under 3 minutes.

---

### Step 1: Opening & Problem Statement (15s)
> *"Today, most healthcare systems rely on disconnected handoffs between physicians and pharmacies. A doctor writes a paper prescription, the patient travels to a pharmacy only to discover the medicine is out of stock, and the doctor is never in the loop. AyuLink transforms prescriptions into a structured digital network."*

---

### Step 2: Doctor Experience — Create Prescription (45s)
1. **Sign in** as **Dr. Arun Menon** (`doctor@ayulink.demo`).
2. Click **"Create New Prescription"** (or use the *Upper Respiratory Tract Protocol* template).
3. Select Patient: **Rahul Krishnan**.
4. Select Recipient Pharmacy: **CarePlus Pharmacy**.
5. Prescribe: **Amoxicillin 500 mg** (1 capsule, 2× daily, 5 days).
6. Note the real-time **Medication Safety Advisory** (Penicillin allergy verification reminder).
7. Click **"Send Prescription to Pharmacy"**.

---

### Step 3: Pharmacy Experience — Stock Check & Notification (45s)
1. Switch to (or open another window with) **CarePlus Pharmacy** (`pharmacy@ayulink.demo`).
2. Open the **Prescription Inbox**. The new digital order for Rahul Krishnan appears instantly.
3. Review the items: CarePlus inventory shows **Amoxicillin 500 mg** is *Out of Stock*.
4. Click **"Notify Doctor (Unavailable)"**.
5. Add note: *"Amoxicillin 500mg currently out of stock at Round North location."* and click **Send Alert**.

---

### Step 4: Doctor Experience — Clinician Decision & Exception Resolution (45s)
1. Return to **Dr. Arun Menon's** portal.
2. An urgent alert appears in the notification bell and on the dashboard banner: *"Action Required: Pharmacy Availability Alert"*.
3. Click **"Review & Resolve Exception"**.
4. Highlight: *"AI assists, but clinicians decide."*
5. Select **"Prescribe Alternative Drug"** and choose **Augmentin 625 mg** (Amoxicillin/Clavulanate) with dosage: 1 tablet, 2× daily, 5 days.
6. Click **"Confirm & Transmit Resolution"**.

---

### Step 5: Fulfillment & Patient Search (30s)
1. **CarePlus Pharmacy** receives the doctor's updated prescription and dispenses it.
2. **Rahul Krishnan** (Patient portal):
   - Views the resolved, updated prescription status.
   - Searches **"Paracetamol 500 mg"** or **"ORS"** to see real-time participating pharmacy availability across Thrissur (CarePlus, GreenCare, Medico).
   - Highlights that **exact inventory numbers remain private**.

---

### Step 6: Demand Intelligence & Closing (20s)
1. Switch to **Demand Intelligence** on the Pharmacy Portal.
2. Point out the **ORS Potential Supply Gap**: High localized search demand (142) vs 46% coverage.
3. **Closing**:
> *"AyuLink doesn't replace the doctor or the pharmacist. It connects them into a safe, synchronized care network."*
